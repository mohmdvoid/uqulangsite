# Code of conduct

## The short version

Technical disagreement is the point of a project like this. Personal hostility
is not.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith.
- Criticise the work, not the author. "This function has a race condition" is
  useful; "this is amateur work" is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline your change, and that they owe you a
  reason rather than an apology.
- Remember that most contributors are writing in a second language. Read past
  awkward phrasing for the technical point underneath.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, including about nationality, religion,
  gender, disability, or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to **conduct@uqulang.com**. Reports are read by the
maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. If your report concerns a
maintainer, say so and it will be handled by someone else.

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, and block accounts that
do not follow this document.
