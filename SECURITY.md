# Security

## This repository — the website

Report a vulnerability in the website (script injection, a broken
Content-Security-Policy, a supply chain issue in the build) to
**security@uqulang.com**. Please do not open a public issue for it.

Include what you did, what happened, and what you expected. A proof of concept
helps; a working exploit is not required.

You will get an acknowledgement within three working days.

## The compiler and toolchain

Vulnerabilities in the uqulang compiler, standard library or toolchain are
handled through your institution's licence support contacts, not here. If you do
not have those contacts, write to **security@uqulang.com** and you will be put in
touch.

## Scope

In scope: this website, the generator in `tools/`, the client-side JavaScript in
`assets/js/`, the security headers in `_headers`.

Out of scope: findings against third-party hosting infrastructure, reports
generated by a scanner with no demonstrated impact, and missing headers that
have no exploitable consequence on a static site with no cookies, no accounts
and no forms.
